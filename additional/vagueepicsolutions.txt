2. Confidence Score Per Task
BreakdownAgent outputs a confidence score alongside each task:
Task: "Add caching layer to dashboard API"
Confidence: LOW — epic was vague, assumption made
Low confidence tasks get flagged on the Review Screen. PM sees exactly which tasks are assumptions vs which are grounded.

3. Assumption Surfacing
Instead of hiding what it assumed, the system shows it:
ℹ️ Assumptions made for this epic:
- "Faster" means < 2s load time
- Frontend-only change, no backend involved
- No auth changes required
PM can correct wrong assumptions directly. Those corrections feed preference memory.
